Why now

Transparency stopped being optional.

Global health authorities have turned guidance into law. The FDA Final Rule, EMA Policy 0070 and the EU CTR, and Health Canada's PRCI all carry hard deadlines and real penalties — and the requirements change constantly.

Most teams answer with a patchwork: a registry tool here, an anonymization vendor there, writers buried in Word, and a spreadsheet trying to track it all. The seams are where deadlines get missed.
